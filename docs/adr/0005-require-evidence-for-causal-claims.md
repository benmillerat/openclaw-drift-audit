# Require evidence for causal claims

The auditor separates Confirmed Cause, Probable Contributor, Plausible Cascade, and Coexisting Drift. A compatibility cascade is represented as evidence-bearing transitions, and every causal edge carries its own provenance and confidence; coexistence or temporal correlation alone cannot establish a root cause. This makes causal reports less decisive when evidence is incomplete but prevents the skill from converting a broad drift inventory into unsupported explanations.
